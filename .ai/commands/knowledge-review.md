# Knowledge Review

Review the current development session and identify
information that should become persistent AI context.

## Execute

Use the `knowledge-maintenance` skill (`.ai/skills/knowledge-maintenance/SKILL.md`).

## Goal

Identify valuable discoveries and classify them into:

- Knowledge
- Rule
- Skill
- Ignore

## Safety

Do not modify persistent context without approval.

Do not persist temporary session information.

Do not silently overwrite conflicting context.
