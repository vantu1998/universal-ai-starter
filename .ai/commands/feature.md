# /feature

Use the `feature-development` skill (`.ai/skills/feature-development/SKILL.md`).

Review the request and drive it through the full workflow: requirements, impact analysis,
implementation, testing, completion.

Follow the `feature-development` skill completely — don't skip a phase because the feature
"looks simple"; use the skill's guidance to decide how much each phase actually needs.

At the end, report:
- What was implemented
- What was tested
- What impact was assessed (modules/APIs touched, risks)
- Anything that needs human confirmation before merging
